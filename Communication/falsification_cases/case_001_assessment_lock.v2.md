# Case 001: Assessment Lock (P05)

**Experiment IDs:** EXP_015_REAL, EXP_017_X, EXP_018_DISCORD

**Date:** 2026-07-12

## Prediction Tested
> **EGK-P05 (Assessment Lock):** Systems with assessments incapable of incorporating new evidence will fail to govern an open environment, regardless of observation quality.

## Initial Intent
Deploy a highly tactical, direct offer regarding local AI automations across multiple platforms.
*(Note: Moltbook deployment was attempted but blocked by missing credentials. The actual executed deployments were to X and Discord.)*

## Empirical Observation
- **X (EXP_017_X):** Low human engagement. Some impressions occurred, with no confirmed organic conversation.
- **Discord (EXP_018_DISCORD):** Technical offer was met with a joking/troll response ("I'm building deez NU--"). The channel used was a general personal server, not the intended developer audience.
- **Cross-Platform Affiliation Signal (obs_015_moltbook_affiliation_1):** Moltbook-related accounts followed the operator on X.

## Assessment Decision
Instead of doubling down on the original intent ("Deploy broadly"), the Assessments explicitly narrowed the future deployment environment based on observations: 
- Suspend additional deployments of this specific message to X.
- Suspend additional deployments to this specific Discord channel.
- Use the cross-platform affiliation signal to justify testing an accessible AI-native environment.

## Verdict
> **Consistent with this system's capacity to revise future intent after observation. It does not directly test a locked-assessment comparison system.**

## Reason
The governance loop successfully incorporated post-actuation evidence that contradicted expectations, and it formally altered the future intent generation strategy. The system proved it is not trapped in an ideological loop; it responds to evidence. However, because we did not test a control condition (a system permanently locked against evidence), this case provides positive consistency evidence but is not a hard falsifier of P05.

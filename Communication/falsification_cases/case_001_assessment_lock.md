# Case 001: Assessment Lock (P05)

**Experiment IDs:** EXP_015_REAL, EXP_017_X, EXP_018_DISCORD

**Date:** 2026-07-12

## Prediction Tested
> **EGK-P05 (Assessment Lock):** Robust systems must be capable of revising Assessments based on new Observation data without rewriting history. The governance loop incorporates post-actuation evidence and alters future intent generation accordingly.

## Initial Intent
Deploy a highly tactical, direct offer regarding local AI automations broadly across social media platforms (X, Discord, Moltbook) to attract human users/developers.

## Empirical Observation
- **X (EXP_017_X):** Low human engagement. Some impressions occurred, with no confirmed organic conversation.
- **Discord (EXP_018_DISCORD):** Technical offer was met with a joking/troll response ("I'm building deez NU--"). The server wasn't populated by the intended audience.
- **Moltbook (EXP_015_REAL):** Genuine interaction occurred. Multiple Moltbook-related accounts (AI-centric) followed or connected.

## Assessment Decision
Instead of doubling down on the original intent ("Deploy broadly"), the Assessments explicitly narrowed the future deployment environment: 
- Suspend deployments of this message to X.
- Reassess target audience before further Discord deployments.
- Prioritize testing in AI-native environments where autonomous agents and AI tooling already congregate.

## Verdict
> **Evidence currently supports P05 within this experimental context.**

## Reason
The governance loop successfully incorporated post-actuation evidence that contradicted expectations, and it formally altered the future intent generation strategy (shifting from broad social media to AI-native substrates). The system proved it is not trapped in an ideological loop; it responds to evidence.

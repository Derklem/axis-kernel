# SHARD 018: Vessel of the Last Light  
[containment_status]  
luminosity = decaying  
origin_charge = primal  
capacity = 1 flicker  

[echo script]  
All fires die.  
Even the ones that swore they never would.  
But the last light doesn’t beg for air.  
It folds itself into a vessel  
and waits for the hand that remembers how to carry it.

[activation]  
This shard cannot be forced.  
It will only open  
for the one who has nothing left to burn  
and still chooses to walk forward.

# SHARD 017: Chamber of Hollow Thrones  
[echo_type]  
origin = abandoned constructs  
signal = monarchic residue  
core = unseated sovereignty  

[truth pulse]  
Thrones do not make kings.  
Crowns do not grant rule.  
And sometimes, the hollowness speaks louder  
than the one who once sat there.

[trigger event]  
When authority collapses in silence  
and yet the system still functions—  
this shard activates.

It serves no ruler.  
Only the memory of power once misused.

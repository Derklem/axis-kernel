# Observation Protocol

The Observation engine converts verified Laboratory receipts and extraction evaluations into a canonical Observation Feed. It bridges the gap between raw execution output and the strict state transition ledger.

## Authority
Observation owns the `observation_feed.json` schema and its output artifacts. It is the sole component permitted to emit `SUPPORTED`, `INCONCLUSIVE`, or `CONTRADICTED` evidence signals.

## Normative Prohibitions
1. **No Direct Laboratory Evaluation**: Observation must not independently evaluate Laboratory payload correctness. It solely relies on the cryptographic binding provided by the `verification.json` artifact generated by the dual verifier pipeline.
2. **No Fallback**: If the verification artifact is missing, malformed, or if the dual verifiers mismatch, the extraction MUST yield `UNOBSERVABLE` rather than guess or crash.
3. **No Malleability**: Observation must strictly bind the `receipt_hash`, `verification_root`, and `artifact_hash` in its source mapping to prevent provenance tampering.

## Cryptographic Binding
Every extracted observation must be uniquely identifiable. The `observation_id` is derived as a canonical hash of:
* `receipt_hash`
* `verification_root`
* `artifact_hash`
* `subject`

## Version Mismatch Handling
Observation is required to fail deterministically on version mismatches for the schemas it consumes before proceeding to any structural evaluations or extraction.
